# Code of conduct

VBAi's issue tracker, pull requests and other project spaces should be useful,
respectful places to collaborate, regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

Discuss technical decisions on their merits. Give actionable feedback, explain
assumptions and accept corrections. Respect other people's time, privacy and
boundaries. Ask before sharing someone else's private messages or documents.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation,
doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Disagreement does
not justify personal attacks. Do not use project spaces for spam or solicitation.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer privately at **init-sys-rev@hotmail.com**, with the subject
`VBAi community concern`. Include relevant links and a concise description. Do not
publish personal information or confront someone on behalf of the project.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Information may be shared when necessary
to investigate a report or protect participants; absolute confidentiality cannot
be promised.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove disruptive content, request a change in
behavior, restrict participation temporarily or exclude a participant. Serious
abuse may warrant immediate action. Decisions should consider the behavior,
context, impact and any repeated violations.

A participant may ask for a decision to be reconsidered using the same private
contact, providing relevant new information. No response deadline is guaranteed.

This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct while explicitly representing
VBAi. It does not authorize contributors to speak on behalf of the maintainer.
